## HCI & ML

The portfolio submission also asks for two written responses detailing your independent research and responses to HCI and ML concepts discussed this term. When writing these responses, consider the following:

- What ml5 model have you chosen in your portfolio response? Why have you chosen it?
- What kinds of interactions do you expect the audience to engage in? How does the ml5 model complement this interaction?
- What is the purpose of the machine learning system(s) you have implemented? How does this align with your interpretation?
- How would you define machine learning with respects to your work?
