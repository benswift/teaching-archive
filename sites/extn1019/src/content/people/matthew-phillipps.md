Hi Creative Computers! I am on the teaching team for the ANU Extension Creative Computing Course in 2023. 

I have a history of creative computing going back 3 decades with [the Australian Centre for Arts and Technology (ACAT)](http://www.avatar.com.au/courses/acat/) - and most recently with the ANU School of Cybernetics [3A Institute (3AI)](https://cybernetics.anu.edu.au/about/about-3a-institute/). I have taught in the ACT College system since 2010 [ - including leading the Global Classroom Project at UCSSC](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2015-06-13/canberra-students-head-global-homeless-forum-scotland/6533532).

I have been involved with curriculum development for extension programs, digital media, 3D animation, algorithmic composition, and the current [Data Science curriculum (Big Data Analytics and Machine Learning)](https://www.bsss.act.edu.au/act_senior_secondary_system/curriculum/bsss_courses/digital_technologies/data_science_a-t-v). I also love samba drumming, cycling, surfing, bushwalking and baking sourdough bread.
