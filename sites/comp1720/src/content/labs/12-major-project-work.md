## Major project

Now's your chance to talk to your tutor about your major project.

**Everybody** should have forked, cloned, committed some work, and pushed their major project by now!

Double and triple check that you have **met the major project specification**.

Read the major project FAQ and make sure you aren't falling into any [major project pitfalls]() discussed in week 11.

## Lab Tasks

To help structure your drop in time, here's some tasks that you can do:

1. Have a 1 on 1 chat with your tutor, show them your Major Project in progress. The best way to show it is on the test server.

2. Show your major project to at least one other student, see how they interact with it without any instructions.

3. Read another student's interaction and artistic statements. Do these statements give you a clear idea about the artwork? Write down some constructive comments for the other student.

4. Upload all your work so far to GitLab. Do all the tests pass? (i.e., do you see green ticks?) If not, think about what might be missing (thumbnail image, references, artistic/interaction statements). Make a start on each part of the assignment so that the tests pass.
