As part of your in-country teaching program, you'll be placed in small groups to
research and present on a topic (of your choice) related to the things you learn
in China. This part of the assessment is run by the BIT organisers of this study
tour.

## Spec {#spec}

You'll be placed in groups when you arrive in Beijing (the students from ANU,
UTS and Swinburne will be all mixed together).

The final presentation will (probably) be on the final morning of the study
tour---Friday Dec 14. They'll give you more details when you arrive in-country.

## Marking

This is worth zero marks (since it's combined with students from the other
institutions, it's too hard to mark fairly).

However, it is a hurdle, as described on the [outline page](/outline/#course-deliverables).

## FAQ

### So will the groups be the same as our "pairs" for the [IoT artefact](/deliverables/04-IoT-artefact/)?

Nope, it's un-related to that.

### So it's really worth zero marks?

Yep.

### What's the point of this, then?

Well, there are a couple of things:

1. what you research & present will no doubt help you with your thinking & ideas
   for the other parts of the course (which are worth something)

2. it's an opportunity to meet some new people---especially to get to know your
   co-travellers from UTS and Swinburne
3. it's part of the content that your hosts at BIT provide, so it'll still be a
   worthwhile learning experience

### Where can I get more details?

They'll tell you all about it when you arrive at BIT.
