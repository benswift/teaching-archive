## Intro to Will's Wonderful World of IoT

#### Self and Blog Introduction
Hi everyone, my name is Will Cashman, and welcome to my blog. This is the introduction to a series of blog posts that will span the duration of my stay in the Beijing Institute of Technology (and a little longer afterwards) where I will be learning about the Internet of Things thematic. In particular, this blog will be where I will document the design and development of an IoT artefact which explores the theme of ‘disconnecting together’.

#### About Myself
I am about to go into my third year of PhB Science majoring in Computer Science at The Australian National University. I actually spent my first year at the ANU in an Engineering degree so though I am thoroughly versed in the foundations of embedded systems, I feel that I am still unfamiliar with many of the facets of computer networks and the broader IoT theme. For this reason (and just general excitement) I am really looking forward to using this experience to fill in as many gaps in my knowledge as possible.

#### Initial Thoughts
Internet of Things is such a broad topic its hard to know where to begin such a project; especially one exploring such an open ended description. Though I will probably wait until I've got a couple of lecture under my belt before I fully commit to an idea, I would say that the facets of IoT that interest me the most are Artificial Intelligence and embedded systems and so I would probably try to combine these. These two topics don't often mix as AI typically demands far more processing power than an embedded systems can provide. But with the advent of cloud computing this is becoming increasingly possible and is something I would love to see realised in a simple, accessible artefact of my design.
