## What is IoT ...

To be honest the word IoT is so widely used across the globe, and yet myself included, not many people understand what it really is. A quick google search defines it as "short for internet of things"... which isn't very useful. But upon further investigation ;) I have found that it is further defined as "the concept of basically connecting any device with an on and off switch to the internet", which can include things like Mobile phones, wearable devices, or even smart fridges.

This got me thinking to what other home appliance could make use of IoT to improve its functionality and help ease everyday life. Throughout my time in Beijing I hope to gain a satisfying understanding of what IoT really is and how it works, and hopefully give me inspiration on an interesting artifact I could work on to help me realise my objective.
