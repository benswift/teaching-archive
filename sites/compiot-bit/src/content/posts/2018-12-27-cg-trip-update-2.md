## Leaving Beijing

After arriving in Sydney from Beijing, the difference in air quality was immediately noticeable. This further motivated our (Adi C and me) idea regarding some sort of air quality monitoring device which can help people who are living or visiting areas which do not have the cleanest air. Some of my classmates on the trip have asthma and they were even more affected by the poor air quality.

## Solution?

### Personal air quality measurement gadget

A device that you can attach to your bag or clothes, which constantly measures the environment around you and builds a heatmap of the air quality around the area by getting data from all the people around the area. The device can also be fed the user's health information so the user can be warned if they're about to enter an area which has especially poor air or an area with pollens/allergens that the user specifically might be affected by. Hence, it can give personalised feedback for the user and potentially even be used to route paths involving the least air pollution.
