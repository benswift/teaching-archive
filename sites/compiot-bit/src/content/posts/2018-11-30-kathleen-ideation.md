## Understand IoT a little bit more

It seems like until now, we still didn't reach the core of IoT concept, (mostly
considering the lectures and practice classes). Thus, I will just conclude
a bit from what I have learnt and talk more detailed than last time
about some of my embryonic ideas for the final project.

For the few days passed by, the main things I learnt are **"Computer Vision"**
and **"Privacy Protection in loT-based Smart Grid"**.

### Computer Vision

I do think the complex **image analysis** process is very interesting and useful:
As I mentioned last time, I hope to monitor some changes in real life and
response to various conditions through IoT knowledge.
I really want to do something close related to our modern city life.

For example, we want to monitor the real-life situation in one specified place,
such as your home. More specifically (just an idea though), for **an intelligent
home security reaction system**, we aim to check whether there is something
unusual happened in your empty home. If thieves in, you can get your
corresponding phone alarm on, and even quick response to open the "supreme”
system for home safety once you confirmed remotely (e.g. deadlocking all locks for
valuable cabinets and safe). If there is a fire, the automatic outfire system can be on.
If your pets suddenly become manic or the elderly left home behaves abnormally,
you will receive the message and can remotely take actions.

In short, you can monitor the other needed intelligent devices at home through
the detected situations. And for the detection method, I think the analysis for
the real-time camera monitor (_i.e. the real-time image analysis through
comparison with big data library in every unit time_) is the core point.

### Privacy Protection

We also need to take privacy into consideration with design. Take
**financial IoT** (my another interested area) as an example.

For instance _(-> it is only a narrow extension)_, we can build _a customer credit
evaluation system_, it will generally collect the customer's financial data from
some usual outstanding parts (emm... still a little ambiguous here...), and
estimate the customer's credit and usual consumption levels.
It will later produce a short-term / long-run customization banking plan for
that person, such as special interest rate, loan amount ... It can both decrease the
bank risk by learning deeply about the customer's payoff ability and attract more
high-credit people with some extra financial benefits.

While the problem is, through the way, there always exist **privacy exposure
concerns**. We can use methods such as anonymous collection to prevent
the _outside-world_ sections, while for the _bank inside_ privacy problems,
it needs more detailed and complex analysis.

## Problems still concerned

I think the main _framework_ for IoT program is that we **collect data**
from real life through **sensors**, **transfer these data through internet**
and **deal with them in our intelligent interface**, finally have some
**application reaction back to real world**.

However, even if I have taken several practice project courses for "sensor" things,
I am still confused _how_ to realize my specific idea:

- What will be the best corresponding "_sensor_" for it? Which kind of sensor?
- What does the _transfer channel_ look like?
- What may our final IoT framework be like? In which kind of way?

I think they may just link to some very basic definitions, while that is really
what confuses me most currently... I do hope that, through my further study in BIT
for IoT, I can have a much clearer framework for the whole project...
