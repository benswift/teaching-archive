## Introduction

Hey everyone, for those of you that don't know me, my name is Sam and I'm a software engineering student, tutor and web developer at ANU. My interest in IoT stems from way back when I got an arduino project kit for christmas quite a few years ago. I had so much fun playing around with it and coming up with ways to tame my universe with automation. It was my first look into embedded devices and electronics, I was surprised how easy it could be to get something cool up and running. As fun and interesting as that arduino was, it wasn't until I got my first network-enabled mini computer (raspberry pi) that the possibilities became endless. Having something that can perform actions, or report back to you from anywhere a network connection exists is the basis that underpins IoT, I'm willing to bet that you could reproduce 99% of the IoT devices on the market today with a raspberry pi and some peripherals.

#### My thoughts on IoT

What makes a good IoT device in my mind is one that provides utility with limited interaction. There are [s](https://press.griffintechnology.com/release/griffin-technology-unveils-griffin-home-a-collection-of-smart-apppowered-appliances-that-simplify-and-enhance-everyday-routines-at-ces-2017/)[o](https://www.amazon.com/Dash-Buttons/b?ie=UTF8&node=10667898011)[m](https://www.indiegogo.com/projects/kuvee-the-smart-wine-bottle-that-keeps-wine-fresh#/)[e](http://www.mysmalt.com) gimmicky devices on the market that do things you can do yourself with minimal effort, the cost and setup of these devices often outweighs the benefit. The best devices in my opinion, are ones that don't require interaction to perform their function, they either provide more of a 'just in case' kind of function, or automate repetitive tasks that can take up a lot of time. There are [m](https://au-en.ring.com)[a](https://www.thetileapp.com/?ref=tiledotcom&utm_source=tiledotcom)[n](https://nest.com/au/)[y](https://store.irobot.com/default/roomba-vacuuming-robot-vacuum-irobot-roomba-i7-plus/i755020.html) great examples of these that monitor and alert, perform automated but easily controllable functions, or just capture data that you can view later and generate statistics from.
