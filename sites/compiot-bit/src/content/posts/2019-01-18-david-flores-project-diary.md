### What i've been working on

While I wait for my shields to arrive, i've been working on connecting the fireabse database to my front-end page which will display all the data from the Infrared Sensor. I have been using the React framework alongside node and ajax, and just testing it out on a localhost through my pc. You can check my progress in my GitHub page under the development branch: https://github.com/davidflorescondezo/office-eye

I have also been working on the front end aspect of the display and have started a trello board to update my progress and stay on task. You can check that out here: https://trello.com/b/8vvpgd2T/officeye

### Problems Faced

The only problem I have faced this week was regarding my order of the shield, I got an email stating that they had accidentally run out of stock of my Wifi-Shield and wouldn't get more in stock for another 2-4weeks. The only thing i could do regarding this was look for another otherwise I wouldn't have enough time to program/work with it. As a result i ended up ordering a cheap Ethernet Shield as opposed to using a Wireless one. I will use this to work for the moment and if the wifi shield arrives early then i will switch back to it alternatively i will have to stay using the ethernet shield.
