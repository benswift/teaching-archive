Hi, I'm Tim I'm a 3rd year comp student at the ANU and this is the start of my china trip blog. I've been to china once before, but I've never been to Beijing, so this should be an interesting trip. I've studied Mandarin as a second language in middle and high school, but it's been 4 years since I've actively practised speaking it, so whilst it will come in handy, I'm hoping I still remember enough to hold a basic conversation. There are also going to be students from University of Technology Sydney & Swinburne University coming along on the trip, so I'll get to meet some new people.

Whilst I've not had much experience with IoT in the past, I really enjoyed the taste of embedded programming I got in the Computer Organisation & Program Execution course that I took at ANU in 2017, so I'll be looking out for more projects like that on the trip. I am a bit worried about the final project that we'll have to do for the course as I've not done well with super open-ended projects in the past, but since it's a group project I should be alright.
