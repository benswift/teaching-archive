### IoT Ideas

I'm still considering what my project is going to be about, and hopefully I'll have a better understanding and sense of direction after I get some exposure at BIT.

Broadly speaking though, I will be interested to integrate IoT with some form of AI/Machine Learning.

My initial idea, which is prone to change, is an animal detection system which uses thermal, motion and some other sensors along with pattern/optical recognition using AI to detect animals during night time. This will help reduce the number of car and animal collisions, which is big problem in Canberra as cars collide with kangaroos.

Another idea is potentially using microprocessors to scan ANU ID Cards automatically so they are easier to check during exams and also makes it simpler to record attendance at events. However, I am not sure how to use IoT effectively to acheive this currently.
