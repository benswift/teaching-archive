Hello everyone! My name is Zoey and welcome to my blog!

## About the Blog

This blog will be about my study at the Beijing Institute of Technology (BIT) on the topic The Internet of Things (IoT) as well as the process of building my personal project themed on (dis)connecting together at the ANU.

## About IoT and Me

Internet of Things is all around us: GPS, home automation, fitness watches, etc. The development of IoT has provided a lot of convenience for human race and I’ve always been looking forward to build one IoT device which will help people the most. The BIT study tour gives such an opportunity to explore my interest in IoT and I am really looking forward to this tour. I am planning to build a simple device which will help as many people as possible, in particular, those with disability, with their everyday life. It would be challenging as this will be my first time building an IoT device and my knowledge about hardware from my previous studies is limited. To overcome this, I hope to learn as much as I can in the BIT study tour and study from each other with the other students in the same tour.

## Off the topic

I am from China and the BIT tour gives opportunity to visit my home country again. I am really excited to show my study tour friends around Beijing and show them the beauty of Chinese traditional culture such as Ancient Chinese architecture, sugar people, Chinese painting, etc.
