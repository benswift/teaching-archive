### IoT in My Mind

IoT stands for internet of things, and it is a very broad topic. One of the aspect of IoT I've found particularly attracting is its capability to extend the dimensions of individual's recognition easily. You can put it anywhere you like as long as it can connect to the internet through wires or wirelessly. It could utilise many sorts of sensors, which means you can now sense your surroundings however you like. This capability of sensing and collecting information altogether is truly amazing.
