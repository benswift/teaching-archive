## Last week to go

It finally goes to the last blog. Three months have passed very quickly. There are lots of enjoyable memories in the tour, and the study and project are very meaningful. 

This is the first time that I have tried to do some research by myself, and put my ideas into practice. The project seems to be a very simple one, but there were still some problems that I haven't expected before and needed to do some search and sometimes guess to find out the reasons. To meet these problems and solve them are challenges. It is interesting that there are lots of times I figured out some problems or got a new way to debug when I was taking a walk. These problems are those that I couldn't solve for the whole previous afternoon. Sometimes walking and relaxation can give people inspiration. 

From this project, the other thing that I have learnt is to do better in self-study. Most of the things in the artefact needs to be built from sketch. And we need to know the usage of the electronics and the way to use and connect them. Luckily we have enough time to learn things and finish the project. It is very fun to build and test things during the whole process. I would like to build more smart home devices to fill my spare time. 

I also learn some critical thinking from this course. When we create something, it is always good to think about why we need them and what can be used. In the beginning, I didn't think anything in-depth about my project. Later when I did more research and looked into more online, I knew that it can be very useful if I can put it in some applications. It is always good to think deeply and clearly before starting the work. 

## What can the artefact do

Now you can go on your holiday without worrying that your plants will be super dry or withered. Just use your phone to check the conditions of your plants and maybe give them some water by just clicking on the phone. They will give you a warm and fresh welcome when you return home. 

## Future project ideas

I got inspired when we were introduced to the applications of IoT in BIT tour. The smart home devices seem to be more and more popular nowadays. Most furniture at home can be connected to the Internet to perform different tasks. The home might be able to clean itself or provide services to people in the future. I want to look into more details about how smart home devices can help people manage their work and live a more comfortable life.
