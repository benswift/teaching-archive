# Intro

Hello! I'm Adi and this is my blog for the _(Dis)Connecting Together_ - China Study Tour. Look out for my weekly posts over the coming months, as I build a prototype using IoT concepts/technologies.

I'm excited to travel to China, particularly looking forward to all the famous sites, especially visiting the Great Wall. I look forward to meeting new people, trying Chinese cuisine and exploring Beijing City.

# Initial Ideas

I am interested in devices that can make repetetive, boring tasks more convenient and easy, as well as devices that can keep us safe. Here are some of the broad ideas I'm interested in and want to explore during the trip to China:

1. Devices that can improve **automobile safety** -- either restricted to single cars or designed for all vehicles on the road -- to reduce accidents and collisions.
2. Designing microcontroller & distributed served based solutions for identity verification, data logging and any similar situations where a small infusion of technology would make our lives more convenient.

I think such devices would make a positive impact on our lives, making it more convenient and safer.

During the trip, I'm also interested in the cybersecurity implications of IoT technology -- there are a lot of risks to connecting microcontrollers on the internet. I'm interested to learn and apply methods to mitigate cyberattacks (like the [Botnet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Botnet) attack) to my own project, and of course, share what I learn with all of you!
