## This Week's Work

The method of how to process the input data from the sensor has been improving in the previous three weeks - both the math function and the data cleaning technique. A few nice plots were taken for comparison of different math functions, before and after the data cleaning filter. Those plots would be included in my design rationale.

This week I also improved the physical connection of the artfact. The female connector part of the wire keeps dropping off from the sensor and in the end I soldered the wire straight onto the sensor to make the connection rigid and safe.

The other thing I did was to hook the math part together with the web part. Will has been developing on the web end and when I pass the "hit" notice to the web, he is able to print out the hit on the web for user to look at.

## Problems Remaining ..

- The math function to distinguish a roll is not always accurate, sometimes it thinks it is a noise and sometime it is defined as multiple hits.
- The conversion from real time hits to music bars and notes is not fully solved - how to decide the note length according to the tempo and the time signature given by the user, etc. (For your interest, the note length in actual time and tempo is related like [this](https://music.stackexchange.com/questions/24140/how-can-i-find-the-length-in-seconds-of-a-quarter-note-crotchet-if-i-have-a-te))
