## Evolving ideas

Welcome back to my blog, this post will be about my extended search for an idea to execute as my artefact.

#### Thinking outside the box

I'm back at the drawing board and I need an idea, so I had been trying to boil down IoT to its simplest form to try and get an overview of what was possible. Good IoT devices provide something useful, automatic or via a simple interaction. That got me thinking, does it have to be useful? Ben had already mentioned that I could go down the data-visualisation route, it would be cool to look at but not particularly useful, and that would be fine.

I also enjoy watching what William Osman and Michael Reeves come up with in their videos (linked in intro post). They are both very creative with what they do and their videos are always entertaining to watch. I began to explore ideas that were not particularly useful but rather had humorous or artistic motivation behind them.

...

So I'm not actually a very creative person and its the end of the year so my brain is is low gear for the time being. I couldn't come up with anything that I found amusing nor anything artistic that was a cohesive enough idea.

Which means, I was stuck...

#### Getting some help

Sometimes you get stuck, it happens to the best of us. Mental blocks, lack of motivation, not seeing the wider picture and many other reasons can contribute to being stuck on something. I've been there before, so I know when its time to ask for help, even a 30 second conversation with another person can be enough to get you going again. This has happened to me before, I've been tearing my hair out over a bug in some code, I go to show it to someone to see if they have any idea and I immediately see the problem. It can be frustrating but those breakthrough moments are the best.

So, I asked for some help.

Friends and family alike, every person I talked to had something to say, which was great! I was mainly looking for inspiration, so I asked them what they would do, or what they would use. I had everything from remote pet interaction systems with treat dispensers, to robots that get beer from the fridge for you. Most were not feasible in the time frame or were just plain silly, but I definitely got a lot out of those conversations.

#### Idea evaluation

Since I now had a decent list of ideas flating around in my head, it was time to start thinking about which ones I could actually execute. It is important to me that what I do is interesting to me and is something I would actually use, otherwise I think motivating myself to do my best in building it may be more challenging. Some of the best ideas that resinated with me were ones that solved problems without having to interact with anything if don't want to. They take a problem that can have a big impact in some way (time, monne etc.) and either prevent the issue from occuring by notifying you, or provide some automated function that tackles that issue.

The two best ones that came from those were:
- **Pool level meter** I'm up in north Queensland at the moment, so nearly everyone has a pool in their back yard. Pools in the sun usually have issues with level because of evaporation. The water level drops down and any tiles or concrete that are exposed can start to degrade in the sun, and it makes controlling the chemical balance more difficult if the amount of water is not consistent. The device that solves this problem could use a level meter to measure how much water had evaporated, and either fill it up automatically if a solenoid is installed, or notify you that it is time to top the pool up with water.
- **Household water usage** With drought and water restrictions so prevalent in this country, it is important to be able to reduce our water usage as much as possible in some places. In addition to this, one of the main causes of household flooding outside of excessing rainfall is water leaks from broken pipes. If these leaks can be identified and stopped, signifacant time and money can be saved as well as minimising damage to homes.

I have spent some time filling in the blanks for each the these ideas to try to pick one. The both intreague me as they fit into this idea of 'Home as a system'. I'll take more about that in my next post.
