## General idea for the project
In my last blog, I mentioned that I wanted to create something that can help me water the plants regularly. After my first week in BIT, I thought more about the project.
Since I want to communicate between items, it would be good to be able to sense the status of one item to then trigger an action response. Here I think I can use a sensor to measure the moisture of the soil. When the soil reaches a certain dryness, another device will be triggered to do perform some actions to dispense water into the plants.  All I will need to do is to fill in a bottle with water which will then be positioned above the plants. The goal is to design the device to pour water into the plants when it senses the soil of the plant is below some threshold value.

## Things that I will likely use
- a sensor to sense the moisture of the soil
- a movable device that can be controlled to open the water bottle
- a bottle of water.
- a computer or microcontroller that can receive the message from the sensor and send message back to the movable controller

## Problems that I am currently concerned about
- Sensor:  I am unsure of what kind of sensor I can use as a moitureing detector, let alone the potential difficulties in sourcing one.
- Connetivity: to connect the sensor, computer, and moveable devices could prove to be challenging.

## Things I am going to do next
I am researching the sensors and movable devices in order to understand how they work and how to connect them to the computer, as well as where to buy them and which ones are most suited to my purposes.
