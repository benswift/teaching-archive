## Ideas

### Ecological Environmental mapping

I was thinking of using some monitoring sensors to mount on birds to try and tackle some environmental mapping issues. This could be especially useful during rescue operations where the environment is unknown and unstable. The birds can be equipped with ultrasonic wave emittor and sensor in an attempt to identify its surroundings and could also potentially be used to help blind birds navigate.

The primary problem associated with the idea is the energy source, as the device mounted on the bird would be required to send out data - as is the requirement for the 'internet' part of 'internet of things'. However, sending data over any sort of network uses a considerable amount of energy and would require some energy harvesting techniques such as:

- Solar Power
- Thermal Energy
- Kinetic Energy
- Wind Energy

However, actual implementation and testing will require considerable effort especially if using an actual bird. To make demonstration and testing easier, a drone could be used instead, however, the amount of energy harvested will probably be different, most noticeably for thermal energy.

### Unlock the Smart Lock

As we were all staying in a hotel during the whole trip, we were all given cards to access our rooms. However, as the doors were autolocked, sometimes people got locked out as they forgot their card in the room or lost it.

To solve this issue, I was thinking of using NFC/RFID technology inside most smartphones these days to unlock the doors instead. This makes it a lot harder to lose or forget your room card as most people always have their smartphone with them. This also makes Check in and Check out a lot more convenient as the whole process could be automated and people don't need to line up at the reception after a long flight. The app could also be used to extend and manage the access to your room to family and/or friends.

The app could also allow safer access to AirBnB homes as homeowners wouldn't need to hide their keys around the house for guests, and risk it potentially being found by unintended parties to gain unrestricted access to their house.

However, security is obviously a huge concern with smart locks and the software would require some serious cryptographic work which my partner, Aditya C, seems quite interested in pursuing.

However, we're still not certain at this point if the idea is fully 'IoT' and would need to discuss with Ben.
