## Yum food
We have been staying in Beijing for two weeks. The biggest problem that I am faced with everyday is that of which restaurant I should go to. There are many different types of Chinese food from many different provinces. I have listed the food that I prefer below. Some of them are located on campus and some of them are from the tourist attractions myself and the rest of the people on the tour have visited.

![food](/images/posts/michelle/food.jpg)

I will introduce them from top left to bottom right.
- Peking duck from Canteen No.7. The ducks are pretty good, and it's quite popular in BIT. Though I think it would be better if they can provide more vegetables because the last two wraps I ate was comprised solely of duck.
- Shrimp hotpot from Xia Chi Xia Shuan. It was also the night that we saw the Kongfu show. The shrimp is very good, but the flavour became very light after adding the soup to the pot.
- Beef rice noodles (*Niu Rou Mi Fen*) from Canteen No.2. It's the second left window in the canteen and there are other kind of meat going with the noodles as well. I like the soup with this noodles very much. It tastes like has been boiled for quite a long time.
- A barbecue restaurant (*Chuan Chuan*) that sells barbecued food with many kinds of meat and vegetables, fried rice/noddles, and covered stew chicken. It is good but the price is higher than the other restaurants on campus.
- Fish noodles from the side canteen attatched to Jinggong Canteen. The place is on the right after passing the drink shop just after entering. The food there is so good and it's my favourite canteen on campus so far. Unfortunately all the menus are in Chinese and there is nothing to point at so non-chinese speaker may find it difficult to order food there.
- A chicken roll from *Nanluoguxiang Hutong*. It seems to be a combination of Beijing food and western food and it's worth a try. But all the food there are pretty expensive.
- The barbecued squid from *Xin Zhong Guan* shopping center. The sauce on the squid is so good and they added some sesame and spring onions. It's also a food that I would highly recommend.
- The best takoyaki!! The seafood in it is very fresh. You can watch the whole process of them making it as well. It is a chain store that I will buy everytime I see it, no matter where I am.
- Taiwan stew meat rice bowl (*Taiwan Lu Rou Fan*). It is in Jinggong Canteen just next to the drink store. It tastes like the common homemade Chinese food.
