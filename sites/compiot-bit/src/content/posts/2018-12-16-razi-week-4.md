# What I've Learned
## Data Processing in IoT
As I've concluded in my previous blog posts, data is essential in any IoT project. We know that data is important and data science techniques can help us in providing the best possible service to the user. We also know that Sensors are typically the medium that provides us with this data.

How then, do we actually go about collecting and processing the data that we want?

Wireless Sensor Networks.

Wireless Sensor Networks, are, what it's name suggests, a network consisting of sensors connected wirelessly. With this wirelessness of the network, it grants the sensors a great amount of mobility. This mobility can be crucial for IoT systems, especially if their system is spread over a large area or may not have plentiful power resources.

Wireless Sensor Networks typically are self organising, cooperative and will require multiple node and cluster heads. Sensors may be placed far away from each other and may not be able to communicate directly to the central node, meaning that several nodes may be placed in between the sensors and the central node to relay the data (Multi Hop).

The way these nodes communicate are through messages. There are several ways to transfer a message:
- Centralised vs Distributed
- Schedule Based vs Contention Based
- Fixed Assignment vs Demand Assignment.
Understanding these different message protocols are highly important in developing a robust and effective sensor network for our IoT device.

## Connected Pet Robot
### Backstory
Growing up, me and my sister had a pretty close relationship. Having lived almost 6 years away from my sister it has been difficult to maintain that close relationship. Sure, we could communicate directly on skype or phone. But being both introverts, talking on the phone is a very extroverted way of maintaining relationships. One difficult thing to emulate in long distance relationships is non-verbal communication and interactions.

I remember me and my sister used to just sit on the same couch doing our own thing but still within close proximity of each other. We didn't say a word to each other, we were in our own worlds, but these kinds of interactions made me feel closer to my sister. These kinds of interactions are hard to emulate via skype or long distance phone calls.

Another common interaction we had was taking care of our cat together. My household always had a cat that became the responsibility of me and my sister. We would feed it together, play with it together and let it join us in our silent couch sessions. One thing we liked to do a lot was pet our cat together (which our cat undoubtedly loved with all four of our hands)

Unfortunately, when I moved away I could no longer have these kinds of interactions.

### The System
So how do I go about sharing this kind of long distance pet ownership? You can't really pet the same pet when the two of you are kilometers apart. Or can you?

No you can't.

But the next best thing is having a mirror image of a pet in two different locations, which emulate a single pet. This way, it can seem like the two of you are taking care of the same pet. This is where the connected pet robots comes in.

The connected pet robots system will consist of two identical pet robots that will communicate over the internet anywhere in the world. It will exhibit typical pet like behaviour which include:
- Needing to be fed
- Needing to sleep
- Wanting to play with the owners
- Reacting to outside stimuli

These behaviours will synchronise over the two pets. Meaning if one pet is sleeping, the other connected pet in a different location will be sleeping. If one owner is petting the cat, both pets, even the one not being physically pet, will be reacting to the pet. This creates the illusion of caring for the same pet (even if physically these two robot pets are different).

One of the ideas I discussed last week was a Data Science related IoT device. My motivation was to collect data about aspects of life, especially ones we have little data on.

Relating this to my previous idea, the pet can collect data about the two owners and their relationships. Long distance relationships are an aspect of life we do not have a lot of data on. For example, the pet can determine when both of you are most active so it the pet can determine what time is the best time to play so that both owners can take part in it.
