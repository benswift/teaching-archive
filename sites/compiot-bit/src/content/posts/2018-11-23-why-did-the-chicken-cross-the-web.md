Now that I've got your attention - I want to talk to you about the Internet. and Things. Internet of Things. But before we delve into that, I just want to quickly introduce you to myself.
My name is Razi, and I am currently a student at the ANU pursuing a Bachelors of Information Technology. I am enrolled in this super cool course (Thank's Ben) taking place in the Beijing
Institute of Technology.

So, Internet of Things or IoT is the concept of embedding interconnectivity among every day objects. It is an emerging field and we can see this with the upward trend of IoT devices around us.
Everyday devices are starting to be connected to the internet, from your toothbrush in your bathroom to the sous vide machine in your kitchen. Big tech companies such as Samsung and Google are experimenting and releasing these sorts of devices to
the market. See the Smart TVs, Google Homes, Amazon Echos, around you.

My experience with IoT is quite minimal. Although the Internet has become a crucial (and at sometimes pervasive) part of my life, I have not yet encountered many IoT devices in the past,
let alone construct one. This is something I am hoping to change with this course.

I personally believe technology is a major agent of change. I am interested in exploring how we could further integrate the internet into our lives for the betterment of people. Although creating a blender connected to the internet
is cool, a more interesting device is one that can significantly improve the lives of people and benefit society. Think smart water meters that help people save water, think smart detectors that help alert people of natural disasters.
Although I do not have a concrete plan yet of what I want to pursue for this course, I am determined to develop something that genuinely benefits people.

So to end this short blog, I'll close with another joke;

How do trees get on to the internet?
