## What did we learn?

We spent the majority of the week finishing up the lab content and working on the Smart Car code and creating the presenting with our group members. The project required us to use light and temperature sensors to sent data from the sensor nodes on the car to the PC node. Unfortunately, we weren't successful in completing the smart car but we worked together to give a great presentation.

The Data Collection and Processing lecture was quite interesting where we gained a wide spread of knowledge on Big Data and Wireless Sensor Networks. We were taught the various levels of architecture. The MAC protocol was also discussed which is the Media Access Control. It is a sublayer of the Data Link Layer in the OSI network model. MAC is responsibe for movement of data flow. The requirements for energy-efficient MAC protocols include collisions, overhearing and idle listening.

I enjoyed the Tranfer Learning in IoT lecture. Some topics that were discussed include problems in machine learning, generalisation, backpropagation, and transfer learning. There is supervised learning and unsupervised leanring within Machine learning. We were taught some components of generalisation error including bias and variance. There can be an underfitting model and an overfitting model. Some ways to reduce variance are to choose simpler classifier, regularize parameters, and get more training data.

## Artefact Ideation

### Past Smart Toothbrushes that have already been developed
There have been some Smart Toothbrushes which have already been made such as the Colgate E1 and Playbrush. They have game nodes for little kids to make brushing a more enjoyable experience. The DiamondClean Smart is for adults only which contain bluetooth connections that is connected to an app which will guide users to brush properly for the right amount of time. This helps to promote better dental hygiene.

### Properties of The Smart Toothbrush
1. Smart toothbrush that will contain motion sensors and ph level sensor ( or heat and light ) to detect cavities in the mouth or other irregulatirites. The motion sensors will keep track of how often a person brushes their teeth and their dental hygiene.

The link below gives some information on how sensors have been developed to detect cavities using pH levels.

http://www.dentistrytoday.com/news/industrynews/item/3575-tiny-sensor-detects-cavities-by-measuring-ph-levels

2. Dental hygiene is not something people want others to know. This is private information being shared which is a form of public shaming something that is very common in China (example number plates displayed of speeding screens, pedestrians photos dispalyed on screens when j-walking) . However, this encourages not to do it so it can also be a good thing. Similarly, if dental routines are shared, then it will force people to maintina healthy dental routines

3. Design factor is important and might be the most difficult task in this project. It needs to be a good size, no sensors causing discomfort when brushing and no microcontrollers poking out etc. Attach sensors to the back of the brush, microcontroller at the bottom, needs to be small, design needs to allow to actually be put into the mouth and to be kid-friendly.

4. The information is sent to a PC next to it. So another node is needed that will take in the data and print it out on the terminal - will show yes there is cavity vs no cavity.
