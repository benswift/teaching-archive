## Launch  
My time spent here has finally come to a close...
The result? A working drum transcribing system which will transcribe the user's drum input in real time and upload the score onto an online server where it can be viewed and shared. 
By the end, every aspect of the project worked well with the exception of rendering more complex musical score on the website which still not operational. 

On the 22nd of Friday, Zoey and I presentated our project at an exhibition during the O-Week on ANU. The project received quite well with several members of the audience approaching us to praise the final product and inquire about how it could be extended later on.

Overall, I am happy with how the project has ended because I think the project will serve to provide a solid foundation for other developers who want to expand the project in different directions. For example, adding more functionality to the website to enable better comminucation between users and options to manipulate the score after they have been submitted. Additionaly, another great feature I would love to see realised it to to make accessable for other instruments, not just drums.

For anyone who is interested in knowing this project a little more, all the files of the project have been published on [GitLab](https://github.com/Wcashman1351/Drum_Transcriber_ASC).
