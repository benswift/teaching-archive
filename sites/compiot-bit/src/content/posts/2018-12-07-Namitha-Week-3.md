This is my blog post for our second week in Beijing.  I will be discussing what I learned this week, some of the activities we did and the development of my ideas for the artefact.

## What Did We Do?

This past week we visited the Forbidden City and hiked up a mountain behind the Forbidden city. The view from the top was gorgeous and really made me love Beijing even more. The Forbidden City had many buildings with a lot of rich history.

This past week we had a couple company visits including SAP and IBM. It was really interesting to see the what the different companies do and we even got to go on a tour of the buildings which was nice. I'd really like to work there someday!

This week has been a bit more relaxed than last week and we've been able to kick back and just watch movies with the others in our rooms. A couple of us went to a Chinese restaurant which was hilarious because we didnt have anyone that could speak Chinese helping us and we were trying to order off the menu but didnt really know what anything was. We were all very clueless when the waitress began to ask us specific questions about our order so we called up one of the student helpers who spoke to the lady over the phone.

## What Did I Learn?

Our first lecture was on Data Science for IoT. We discussed ways Data Science and IoT are linked and how big data can be used in the field of data science. Big data is a term that is applied to data sets with a size beyong the ability of commonly used software tools to capture manage and process data with tolerable time.

The lecture on cloud computing really expanded my knowledge on cloud computing which is an internet based access model that applies virtualisation on data centre technologies. We also learned the various data centre architecture layers which include the physical layer, system layer, application and lgorithm layer. Some applications of cloud data centre include smart houses, drones, and video monitoring.

We also continued to work on our smart car this past week and learned how to program the temperature and light sensors on the arduinos to print out the values on our laptops. We will be moving on to using these skills to program the sensors in our own smart cars next week which we will then have to present.

We also had a chinese calligraphy class where we were taught how to write various chinese characters on a special piece of paper. The classes where we explore and deepen our understanding of the chinese culture are very interesting and i've been enjoying them a lot because it has allowed me to appreciate the chinese culture.

Finally, we had a guest lecture on International Business and the Chinese Market which gave us a basic overview of the Chinese economy.

## Artefact Ideas

After exploring different topics around the cloud system in the lectures this week, I want to incorporate video monitoring into my project. This can be done with my toothbrush idea by adding a tiny camera in the toothbrush which will then be connected to the dentists computer system too allow dentists to view the irregularities within the mouth and make judgements about the patients issue even before they actually go to the dentist for a check up. The heat and light sensors can be used to detect any initial stages of tooth decay, etc. and then this can be further looked at by the dentist through the video camera embedded within the toothbrush. The information will need to be sent over with some sort of cloud system.
Smart houses and drones were also discussed in lectures this week which i'd be interested in pursuing as well.
