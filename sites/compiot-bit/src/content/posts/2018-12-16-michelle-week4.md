## BIT IoT trip
Three weeks passed very quickly, the trip is end and everyone is heading back and leaving Beijing. It is a great tour with lots of nice friends and wonderful experience. The last day's presentation is pretty good for everyone, except that everyone seems lack of sleep due to the early morning that we need to check out. There are many awesome ideas introduced in the presentation and lots of interesting discussions.

During these three weeks, we had a lot of introductory lectures. There are some lectures that are very impressive, like the privacy, cloud, machine learning, deep learning. and data science.

The Chinese cultural classes are also very funny. It is interesting to see some of us put some innovative ideas in Chinese painting and calligraphy. From my previous thoughts these activities are very traditional and classical, but when they combine some other characteristics, they become more alive and vivid to me. Like I have never seen someone used Chinese painting tools to draw an animate before. It is pretty interesting.

For the exploration part, I have been to most of them around 10 years ago. The same thing is that we still need to stand in the queue for a long time to visit every tourist attraction. The different thing for me is that now I have a stronger feelings when I see the places. From the history that we have learnt in middle and high school in China, I know about some stories that happen in some places. When I revisit them, I will think of the event that happened before, just in the exact same place. It is an amazing feeling to imagine the scene that how the event happened at that time and how was it looked like. And more amazing to find that it wasn't a long time ago and things can be changed very quickly. It is really good to have the opportunity to go to all these places and experience more Chinese culture.
