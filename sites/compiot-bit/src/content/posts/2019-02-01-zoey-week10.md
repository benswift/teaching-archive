## Closer to Efficiency - or not?

Polling or using interrupts? This has always been the question when people are talking about efficiency and interrupts are always favored. Mentioned in [Polling vs. Interrupt] (http://www.electronics-base.com/useful-info/software-related/90-polling-vs-interrupt) by Vladimir Milosavljevic

> There are many advantages of using Interrupts.
> The microcontroller can serve many devices.
> Each device can get service based on the priority assigned to it.
> The microcontroller can ignore (mask) a device request.
> The use of microcontroller is more efficient.

Interrupts are always favored because they are more efficient and can serve more devices. In our case, the number of drums is not large and polling is actually more efficient than using interrupts - it takes time for interrupt handlers to jump in and out the program too!

## Less Noise, YAY!

Noise is the problem that many people have been working on to reduce.

### Preamplifier

One of the method is using preamplifiers. There is one [paper](https://www.metrolog.net/files/piezofilm_noiseperformance_en_metrolog.pdf) about using preamplifier over minisense 100 (the sensor we are using), written by R H Brown.

### Filter

The other method is by using low-pass/high-pass filters to filter out the noise.
