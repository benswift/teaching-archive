## Understanding of IoT
I haven’t learnt anything about IoT before. But I do know that we are exposed to IoT quite a lot. Many common everyday devices and emerging technologies such as vending machines, sweeping robots, shared bikes, self-driving cars, and some smart home automations like Google home. By using sensors, items can communicate with other items to create a cohesive system which can automate many common tasks people would otherwise have to perform. This automation can often achieve a greater level of efficiency and quality that humans can provide, as well as freeing people's time to perform other more complex tasks.
