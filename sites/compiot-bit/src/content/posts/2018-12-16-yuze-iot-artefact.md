### IoT Device for Checking Presence and Absence During Group Activity

If you checked out my post from last week, one of my sub-title is "Where is Jason" and yes it is the reason for me to commit to this IoT Artefact. It is very inefficient and unprecise for the group leader to figure out who's missing and who's not manually. The existing mechanism, such as bind to roommate or check by best mates, all have significant limitations and could easily miss someone. But if we assign each of the group member a RFID tag and give a master device, which can scan be used to scan and see who's present, to the group leader. The whole process would be much more efficient and people can spend more time on meaningful stuff.

However, the scale of the device functionality is worth discussing. Currently my main consideration would be the cost of the whole device, since the cheaper it is, the easier it could be utilised in more scenarios. For example, if the cost for each FRID tag is just $0.50, that would be much acceptable by any sort of activity group. However, the functionality of RFID tag is quite obvious. It could not actively report who has been dropped hence the group leader won't be able to find it out as soon as possible. Another drawback would be the security of RFID is quite limited. Someone could just disguise as one of the group member by scan one of tag and make one by himself. They could easily pretend someone is present and secretly kidnap them and slip away before the group leader could recognize (in a movie that is).
