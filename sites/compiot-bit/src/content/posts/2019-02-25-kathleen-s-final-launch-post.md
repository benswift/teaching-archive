## Further Thinking for My Artefact and Theme

In my [design rationale](https://cs.anu.edu.au/courses/china-study-tour/news/2019/02/18/kathleen-s-design-rationale/), I've put some efforts to talk about my understanding for IoT through the summer research process, the project inspiration and background, how does my project connect to our social real life, some critical further thinking and possible improvement points to the artefact...

I do think with my artefact contributes to *"connecting together"* **theme**:
- *connect pets with tech*: Tortoises (or turtles), the special pet species which are always ignored in traditional pet feeding devices, can enjoy the latest technique, and thus enjoy better (more scientific) life.
- *connect human with pets*: Obviously, the artefact could help you learn more about your pet real-time living environment - through sensors' data and actuators' working condition. Also, it could help you take care of your tortoises (with some website remote control) even if you are away from home.

Although I feel happy currently that my artefact reaches even all my initial idea for various functions, I never consider it as a substitute for human being owners~ It's just an additional super helper for tortoises' life! 

It's the same idea as a nanny's professional care could never replace love from clumsy parents. For owners, (at least for me,) nothing could replace real touch feeling and owners couldn't be more relieved to stay beside their pets. For pets, they are brilliant enough to feel everything - e.g. how much care you give to them... In addition, if I do not know that much about tortoises' habits through my long feeding experience, I'll have no idea about the function design (especially for the "land-water swap" one). It all comes from real life~

I know that we'll all have some more important things to do. Same to me. While at least, give some time to stay with them... Real feeling is better than any critical thinking.

I highlighted several times that, my artefact targets are actually tortoises, rather than human beings. (It's a trade-off.) Although it may make people feel a little hard to understand all making principles behind functions without any explanation, tortoises can enjoy life with the pre-set rigorous logic. I think the owners will feel satisfied then as well~

Overall, as the summer project is already completed enough to show all my general idea for each section and I also couldn't find any similar "smart tortoise home" device in market~ I really hope it could have some further development, *if having chance*, and make some efforts~
