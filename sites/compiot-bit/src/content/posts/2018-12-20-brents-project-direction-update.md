## Changes

### Project Direction
Due to some recent developments, I've decided to alter the course of my project away from long distance connections and into other areas.  
I'm currently in the generation stage but I have been briefly skimming some ideas relating to:
* extending RF based applications and abstracting them across the internet
* using [robots](https://www.anki.com/en-us/vector) to help bring a person into a room to participate in group activities
* creating an IoT security based headset to protect audio based communication in [light of the new bill](https://www.aph.gov.au/Parliamentary_Business/Committees/Joint/Intelligence_and_Security/TelcoAmendmentBill2018)
* creating a small buddy device that alerts you every time your phone makes a connection to a ['big 4' company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Four_tech_companies)

I hope this post helps clear up any confusion on the lack of flow and direction between my last blog post and the next, which will involve a pitch for at least one of my new ideas so stay tuned!
