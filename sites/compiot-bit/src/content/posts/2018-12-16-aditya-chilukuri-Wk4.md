## Main Idea Discussion

At this stage, I am keen to work on the Smart Lock Idea for hotels and apartments. I have looked for some readings about zero-knowledge proofs (ZKPs), which are given [here](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/courses/archive/fall07/cos433/lec15.pdf), and the [paper](https://groups.csail.mit.edu/cis/pubs/shafi/1985-stoc.pdf) where ZKPs were first described. From the mathematical/theoretical perspective, I would be excited to take this project in the direction of crypography and proofs of identity problems — but nothing is set in stone yet as I am keen to work in a pair and need to discuss this with Chinmay.

I'll try to make a blog post in the middle of this week following up on our idea, and also a condensation/analysis of the cryptography study I've linked above.
