**Ideation**

>The **Internet of things** (**IoT**) is the network of devices, vehicles, and home appliances that contain [electronics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronics), [software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software), [actuators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Actuator), and [connectivity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_access) which allows these things to connect, interact and exchange [data](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data).[[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_of_things#cite_note-Linux_Things-1)[[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_of_things#cite_note-Linux_21OSP-2)[[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_of_things#cite_note-ITU-3)[[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_of_things#cite_note-4)
>
>IoT involves extending [Internet connectivity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_access) beyond standard devices, such as desktops, [laptops](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laptop), [smartphones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smartphone) and tablets, to any range of traditionally *dumb* or non-internet-enabled physical devices and everyday objects. Embedded with technology, these devices can communicate and interact over the [Internet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet), and they can be remotely monitored and controlled.
>
>-- <cite>Wikipedia</cite>

Above is the definition of IoT from Wikipedia. What's the potential of IoT ? It makes traditional devices much more configurable and could be easily installed. IoT is about sensors and wireless connectivity.

How about having a simple navigation device within the subway system. It's handheld, and we would upload destination onto the IoT device when we bought the ticket. And then it would just tell you which direction, which line, stay in the train or leave, which line we are switching to. Or actually we could have a similar device for traveling around in the Summer Palace. It just show you the suggested direction (in a real-time flavour) for a amount of time. It could be group base so that when the group are far away from each other. You can tap a button and it would show a direction where to go to catch them up. (Based on walkie talkie technology, hence there is no hassle to bind it with your mobile phone hence more robust). Maybe we could add a keyboard onto it so that we can broadcast messages instead of just voices.

We can deploy an sensor at the outside of the window that is connected to your phone app/wechat account so that it would remind you to take the mask with you.

I used to have an idea of distributed sensor network that would collect data and then convert it into music by using data sharing the same timestamp in the same bar.

Baggage tracker...probably not... for luggage claiming in the airport...not very suitable.

Liquid Level Monitor -- maybe a humidity monitor for a vase. It could remind the owner to water the flower. (widely distributed humidity sensor.

Having a device that indicates air quality, such as the density of carbon dioxide and the air pollution index, could be helpful. Personally, I am very sensitive to the density of CO2. Once it reaches a certain threshold, I would become really sleepy and couldn't concentrate anymore. Having a small sensor around with me to detect the CO2 density of surrounding environment would be useful to me since it could directly inform me the status of surrounding and I can react immediately rather than wait until the syndrome happened.
