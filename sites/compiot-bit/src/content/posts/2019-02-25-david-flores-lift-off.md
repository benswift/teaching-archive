### Release
[**OfficEye**](https://github.com/davidflorescondezo/office-eye) is a combination of an IoT device/Application developed by David Flores-Condezo. OfficEye aims to increase worklpace productivity and by motivating employees to work more effiently by displaying real-time statistics of each employee's working hours/minutes/seconds. OfficEye also has a special portal for the employer that will enable them to have hands on access to what every individual employee is currently working on through computer application oversight. More info on [GitHub](https://github.com/davidflorescondezo/office-eye)

If you're interested in knowing more about how the project and ideas were developed, check out my [Design Document](https://cs.anu.edu.au/courses/china-study-tour/news/2019/02/18/david-flores-design-document/). Alternatively you may also check out my weekly [Blog Entries](https://cs.anu.edu.au/courses/china-study-tour/news/#david-flores-condezo) documenting the project's progression step by step.

### Retrospective/Limitations(Ways to "hack" OfficEye)
Although I have developed a well rounded product, it is not completely "hackproof" and the application still has a few kinks that need to be solved. The infrared sensor is vulnerable to things like fans or remote moving objects however some of these are countered by the application and although it is not fullproof it already counters many ways of unproductivity and as a side project I will try to increase it's efficency.
