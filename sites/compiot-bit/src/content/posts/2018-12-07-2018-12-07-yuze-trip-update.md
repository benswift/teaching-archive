**Deeper Thoughts of IoT Artefact**

"Where is Jason ?"

No one knows when did Jason get left behind. Literally, no one. And it turns out that Jason has stayed in the subway station for 2 hours hoping we are going to pick him up. That's really rough.

And this is definitely not the second time. And it is very inefficient to solve out this problem. I think a distributed IoT device might become handy in this scenario.

Here's my initial thought. I want to prototype a button-sized IoT artefact that would utilise low energy Bluetooth (so that the battery could use longer) to solve this issue. If we assign each of the group members with a device, they would form a wireless LAN via Bluetooth and what they are trying to communicate with each other would be really simple -- the group of devices would just maintain a table which would record all the device id that are currently present. If we need to check whether everyone is present, we can pull the table from one of the device and examine the data at mobile phone/laptop.

I would look further into Bluetooth communication, especially power consumption and transmission range. Personally, I think it won't require much processing power. The hard part would be configuring Bluetooth and hook up the system with a battery. Shrinking size of the device would be out of scope for this project so I would mainly focus on the battery life. A month of battery life would be really decent, but a week won't be too bad.
