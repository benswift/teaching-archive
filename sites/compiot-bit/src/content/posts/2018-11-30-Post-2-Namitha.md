# Week 1 in Beijing

## What did we do?

Our first week in Beijing was amazing. I made so many new friends, explored different parts of this city, learned a bit of Chinese, and tried some Chinese food. Our days usually consist of classes during the day and then exploring different parts of Beijing at night.

On the first day, we explored the BIT campus and we had our first lecture. We went to Wangfujing Street the first night to have some traditional chinese street food and some of us went shopping as well. The shopping areas are so different to the ones in Canberra. It was so nice to see all the crowds and lights coming from Canberra. The next night, a couple of us went to a shopping area called The Place which is another big shopping street in Beijing. We toured the Summer Palace which had a breathtaking view. The walk was a bit too long but we had fun regardless.

The hardest part about coming here is definitely trying to understand the language. Most people here don't understand English so having some Chinese friends with us on this trip is really helpful. The other shocking thing was the air pollution because coming from Canberra, many of us found it quite difficult to adjust the first few days. Wearing a mask definitely helped. Chinese food is very different to Australian food so initially I wasn't too fond of the different Chinese dishes we tried. However, i'm beginning to like the food a lot more now.

## What did I learn?

Our first lecture was on Computer Vision and Vision Big Data. The goal of computer vision is to perceive the 'story' behind the image. It can be used for robotics, internet vision, augmented reality. It can be used with security, health, and surveillence. We learned about reconstructing images to objects and extracting particular parts of a image and actually making sense of it. This was then related to machine learning and deep learning.

Another topic that was introduced to us was privacy in smart grids. We were taught about intrusive load monitoring and non-intrusive load montioring. We discussed possible was to privacy in smart grids and ways to protect it from hackers. The lecturer went on to explain the Laplace distribution. We were also taught the basic concepts of networks which I thought was a good refresher on everything we have learned in the past.

We also had classes on the Chinese language and Chinese culture. I found this very interesting because it gave us a break from the other course content and at the same time teaching us skills that we can use in the next two weeks. We were taught some basic Chinese phrases and we also spend some time making Chinese crafts.

## Artefact Ideation

After my first week here in Bejing, I have developed a couple more ideas for my project by talking to other people on this trip with me, through the lectures, and from the intelligence tour. The university took us on a very interesting tour to show us some of the research projects that are currently being developed a the Beijing Institute of Technology. After the first week here, We also started working on our Smart Car project which gave me some idea on how communication works between two nodes. I will be using this to further extend my project. I know that I will develop a project that combines Internet of Things with the medical field but still don't really have a fully fledged out project idea.

At the moment, I still think that I might try to extend the toothbrush project which can use the concept of Internet of Things to develop a toothbrush that can detect abnormalities in the mouth and send information to the nominated dentist. This would be very useful because it will keep patients informed about their dental health and just help people have healthy gums and teeth. Sensors can be attached to the toothbrush which will be able to detect any decay of teeth.
Some things that I still need to further look into:

1. How will I actually send the information?
2. What type of sensors can I use?

Something else that I would like to develop would be to create some sort of air filtering device for Chinese people in Beijing. The air is heavily polluted here and the majority of the people use masks to protect themselves from the harmful air. However, I don't believe the mask provides 100% protection. The deivce could also contain sensors which gives people the level of pollution at any given time. This idea is still in its intial stages and i'm still figuring out how it can be extended to incorporate Internet of Things.
